## Superpowers spec/plan location

Design specs and implementation plans (normally written to `docs/superpowers/specs/` and
`docs/superpowers/plans/` by the brainstorming and writing-plans skills) go to
`.superpowers/specs/` and `.superpowers/plans/` instead. That directory is gitignored —
these are internal working docs, not meant to be visible to every repo collaborator.
Do not commit anything under `.superpowers/`.
