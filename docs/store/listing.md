## Description (both stores)

Find live pickup basketball near you.

pull-up is a map of basketball courts that shows who's playing right now.
Check-ins are verified at the court and expire on their own, so a busy pin
means a real run — not a stale post from last week.

- LIVE MAP — courts near you with live player counts
- CHECK IN — one tap when you pull up; auto check-in optional
- CROWD REPORTS — "~8 playing, good run" from people at the court
- PLAN RUNS — set a time, see who's in, get pinged when your court has a run
- COURT CHAT — talk with the regulars at your court
- ADD COURTS — the map is community-built on OpenStreetMap data

No ads. No tracking. Location is only used at the moment you check in.
